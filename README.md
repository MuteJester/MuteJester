### Thomas Konstantinovsky

PhD candidate working on algorithms, probabilistic models and machine learning for immune receptor sequences (BCR and TCR repertoires).

**Main projects**

- **[AlignAIR](https://github.com/MuteJester/AlignAIR)**: deep-learning aligner for immunoglobulin and T-cell receptor sequences. Allele calls, segment boundaries and the junction from a single model. [Nucleic Acids Research, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf651)
- **[GenAIRR](https://github.com/MuteJester/GenAIRR)**: simulator of adaptive immune receptor sequences with full ground-truth annotations, across 23 species. [Briefings in Bioinformatics, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbae556)
- **[LZGraphs](https://github.com/MuteJester/LZGraphs)**: compression-based graph models of immune repertoires for generation probability and diversity, without sequence alignment. [Bioinformatics, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btad426)
